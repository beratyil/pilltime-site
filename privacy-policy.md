# Privacy Policy for PillTime

**Last updated:** June 15, 2026

PillTime ("the App") is built with your privacy as a core principle. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how.

## Summary

- PillTime does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data.
- PillTime works entirely **offline**. It has no servers, no user accounts, and does not require an internet connection.
- All data you enter (medication names, doses, and reminder times) is stored **only on your device**, using Apple's standard local storage.
- PillTime does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies of any kind.

## Information You Provide

When you add a medication, you may enter:

- A medication or supplement name
- A dose description
- One or more reminder times

This information stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. Neither we nor any third party has access to it.

## Notifications

PillTime uses Apple's local notification system to remind you about your medications. These reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. No notification content is sent to any server.

## Health Information

PillTime does not connect to Apple Health (HealthKit) or any other health platform. Any health-related information you enter (e.g., medication names) stays exclusively within the App on your device.

## Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you can remove it at any time by:

- Deleting individual medications within the App, or
- Deleting the App from your device, which removes all associated data.

## In-App Purchases

If PillTime offers optional in-app purchases, these are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. PillTime does not collect or store any payment information.

## Children's Privacy

PillTime does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

## Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
